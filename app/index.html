Missable trophies — what “missable” means here

Every flag below is built from the official trophy descriptions alone. A trophy is flagged missable when its description ties it to one named story mission, chapter, or strand finale — a moment you play once. Everything else can be earned in free roam after the story. If a mission run goes wrong, restart it from the pause menu rather than moving on.

17 trophies are missable by that rule. Earn them on your first pass through the story and the rest of the list is safe.
